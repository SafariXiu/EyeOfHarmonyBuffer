EyeOfHarmonyBuffer includes code and shaders ported from:

  Orbital Railgun (orbital-railgun)
  https://github.com/Mishkis/orbital-railgun
  Copyright (c) 2025 Mishkis
  Licensed under the MIT License.

Ported portions (Java logic, GLSL shaders, timing/visual design):
- ItemOrbitalRailgun (item behaviour)
- OrbitalStrikeManager (server-side strike state machine)
- RailgunClientState (client-side state machine, based on RailgunState)
- RailgunClientEvents (client event handling, based on ClientEvents)
- RailgunWorldRenderer (strike/aim visuals, based on the original renderer)
- shaders/gui.fsh, shaders/strike.fsh, shaders/chromatic_abjuration.fsh
